001 — how it works

See it, open it, talk to it.

01see all your work at a glance

Every repo and feature as a dot. Click one to drop into its sessions.

02watch a feature's workers

Each feature has an orchestrator and numbered workers: scout, scope, build, verify, review, ship.

03talk to any worker

Type and press return. Your text is sent to the worker, and queues while it is busy.

in the box
  1. 001a samara command — everything in the box is also a command
  2. 002several claude logins — map repos to accounts
  3. 003extensions — add your own rows to the box
  4. 004feature notes — ⌘O the note, ⌘P the last PR
  5. 005voice input — press ⌘M, say the prompt
  6. 006your terminal — Terminal, iTerm, Ghostty or cmux
